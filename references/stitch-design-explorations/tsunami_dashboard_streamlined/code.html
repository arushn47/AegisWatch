INTERGOVERNMENTAL TSUNAMI DIRECTIVE
1. Natural Warning Signs visibility

Do not await civil sirens if shore anomalies are directly observed. Nature provides zero-delay cues:

  • check_circle Rapid Ocean Recession: Abrupt drainage exposing reefs, sea floor, and beached fish.
  • check_circle Severe Ground Tremor: Earthquake shaking that exceeds 20 seconds or prevents standing.
  • check_circle Sub-Audible Acoustic Roar: Deep rumble resembling heavy oncoming train or jet engine.
ACTION: EVACUATE SHORE IMMEDIATELY ON SIGNS
2. Immediate Evacuation nordic_walking

Vertical displacement is life critical. Tsunami waves move faster than humans can run:

  • warning Reach Terrain Elevation: Ascend immediately to hills, terraces ≥30 meters above sea level.
  • warning Designated Vertical Towers: If no high ground exists, enter concrete engineered shelters >4th floor.
  • warning Never Spectate Wave Arrival: Consecutive crests follow 10-45 minutes later and can be larger.
ACTION: DO NOT RETURN UNTIL OFFICIAL ALL-CLEAR
